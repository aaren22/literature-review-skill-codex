# Synthesis vs Summary — 关键差别与改写

> **核心 Source:** Torraco, R. J. (2016). Writing integrative literature reviews. *HRDR*, 15(4). DOI: 10.1177/1534484316671606

---

## 一句话区分

- **Summary（堆砌）：** A 说 X，B 说 Y，C 说 Z。
- **Synthesis（综合）：** 关于问题 P，已有研究可分为 X 派（A）与 Y 派（B,C），他们在 Z 上分歧，这暗示 W。

**Synthesis 的三要素：**
1. **归类** — 把研究分组
2. **对比** — 指出组间异同
3. **意义** — 说出这意味着什么

---

## 对比示例

### ❌ Summary（堆砌）

> "Smith (2020) studied the effect of A on B using experiment, finding
> a positive correlation. Jones (2021) examined the same relationship
> using survey, also finding positive. Wu (2022) did meta-analysis and
> reported mixed results. Chen (2023) argued that moderators matter."

---

### ✅ Synthesis（综合）

> "The effect of A on B has been examined across methods, with
> **broadly positive but inconsistent findings**. Experimental (Smith
> 2020) and survey studies (Jones 2021) reported positive correlations,
> whereas Wu's (2022) meta-analysis revealed **substantial heterogeneity**
> (I² = 68%). This inconsistency has been attributed to **moderator
> effects** (Chen 2023), particularly [variable X]. **This suggests
> that studies assuming a main-effect model may miss important
> conditional relationships**—an issue our research directly addresses
> by including [具体 moderator]."

---

## Synthesis 的 7 种招式

### 1. 对比（Comparison）

- "Whereas X argues A, Y argues B."
- "In contrast to X's approach, Y uses..."
- "X and Y diverge on C."

### 2. 归类（Categorization）

- "Research can be grouped into three streams: (1)..., (2)..., (3)..."
- "Two dominant perspectives have emerged..."

### 3. 演进（Progression）

- "Early work (X, Y) emphasized A; more recent studies (Z) shift toward B."
- "The field has moved from C to D."

### 4. 冲突（Conflict / Debate）

- "Findings are inconsistent..."
- "There is ongoing debate about..."

### 5. 整合（Integration）

- "Despite different methods, these studies converge on..."
- "Taken together, these findings suggest..."

### 6. 因果（Causal Linking）

- "The inconsistency may stem from..."
- "This pattern can be explained by..."

### 7. 含义（Implication）

- "This suggests that..."
- "The implication is that..."
- "Together these point to a gap in..."

## 常见 Summary 陷阱与改写

### 陷阱 1: "作者接龙"

❌ "Smith (2020) says X. Jones (2021) says Y. Chen (2022) says Z."

✅ "Research converges on [共同点] but diverges on [分歧点]. Smith (2020) and Jones (2021) both found X, attributing it to [机制]. Chen (2022), however, reported Y, arguing [不同机制]."

---

### 陷阱 2: "引用墙"

❌ "Many studies have shown X (A; B; C; D; E; F; G; H)."

✅ "Strong evidence supports X across [方法 1] (A; B) and [方法 2] (C; D), with consistent effect sizes (β ≈ 0.3-0.5). Exceptions (E; F) are attributable to [context]."

---

### 陷阱 3: 原文照搬

❌ Direct quote 多于 paraphrase。

✅ 90%+ paraphrase，10% 以内 quote（只在关键概念定义 / 无法改写时用）。

## 改写练习（从 summary 到 synthesis）

**原 summary：**

> "Li (2018) used machine learning to predict student dropout with 82%
> accuracy. Wang (2020) used deep learning with 87% accuracy. Zhang
> (2022) used transformers with 91% accuracy."

**改 synthesis：**

> "Prediction accuracy for student dropout has **steadily improved
> with model complexity**: from traditional machine learning (Li 2018,
> 82%) to deep learning (Wang 2020, 87%) to transformer-based models
> (Zhang 2022, 91%). However, **all studies share a key limitation**:
> models are trained on single-institution data, raising concerns
> about generalizability. **Our research addresses this by**
> [具体方案]."

**改动：**
- 加"steadily improved"做 synthesis
- 归类为演进
- 加**共同局限**
- 加本研究 contribution

## Synthesis 段的 5 类主题句

1. **"The literature reveals [pattern]..."**
2. **"Research on X can be grouped into [N] streams..."**
3. **"Despite progress, a gap remains in [领域]..."**
4. **"Findings are [consistent / inconsistent] on X..."**
5. **"The evolution from X to Y reflects [change]..."**

## 批判维度清单

每段综述可选 1-3 个批判维度：

- [ ] 样本代表性（WEIRD sample / 单一群体）
- [ ] 方法局限（设计 / 测量 / 统计）
- [ ] 理论假设（未检验的前提）
- [ ] 时代局限（数据过时）
- [ ] 地域局限（仅某国 / 某文化）
- [ ] 学科局限（单学科视角）
- [ ] 操作化分歧（同概念不同测量）
- [ ] 生态效度（实验室 vs 现实）

---

## 自审 Checklist

综述段写完问：
- [ ] 段落有明确 synthesis 点（非 summary）？
- [ ] 用了对比 / 归类 / 演进等 synthesis 招式？
- [ ] 引用数量合理（3-7）？
- [ ] 有批判立场？
- [ ] 段末 / 节末有 gap 暗示？
- [ ] 主题句独立可读？
